## When you email us

If you email us, for example to book a demo, we use your name, email address and message to reply and to follow up on your request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry, or steps before entering into a contract you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(b)). We keep the correspondence for as long as the conversation or a resulting business relationship needs it.
